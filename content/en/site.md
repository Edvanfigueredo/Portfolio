## About

**H1:** From regulated operations to technology consulting

**Where I started.** My working life began in 2013 at Hospital Dom Pedro de Alcântara in Feira de Santana, Brazil. I started as an administrative apprentice, then moved into medical records, nursing and the oncology pharmacy [C12]. In oncology, preparation, traceability and stock control leave no margin for error. It taught me to respect process, data and the people who depend on both.

**Leading people before leading systems.** I led the hospital's medical records team, and later supervised a store team at Droga Raia (RD Saúde) [C13]. Those roles taught me what technology projects often underestimate: adoption, routines, and the people who keep a process running every day. From 2021 to 2026 I was a nursing technician at Rede D'Or and a daily user of Tasy, a hospital ERP. I saw how systems can help people doing critical work, and how they can get in the way.

**Retraining, deliberately.** Alongside hospital shifts, I completed a technology degree in Systems Analysis and Development (2024–2026) [C8], started a bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, and earned Microsoft's Azure, Azure Data and Power Platform Fundamentals certifications.

**Today.** Since March 2026 I have worked in technology consulting at EY, in the AI & Data practice for financial services [C2]. Banking and healthcare share more than it seems: regulation, sensitive data, audit trails and very low tolerance for failure. My operational background is part of how I approach that work.

### How I work
- **Say what is proven.** I separate what is planned, prototyped, tested and validated, in code and in conversations.
- **Process before tools.** I understand how the work actually happens before choosing technology.
- **Leave a trail.** Clear documentation, traceable decisions, small verifiable steps.

### Current focus
- Generative and agentic AI: retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent orchestration, evaluation
- Data architecture and analytics on Azure
- Financial services domain knowledge
- Professional English for international work [C14]

### Direction
In the next few years I want to become a reliable reference for AI and data engineering in financial services. Longer term, I want to grow into technical and delivery leadership, building on the people leadership I practiced before technology.
